# Data requirements and scope

Primary development scope: ice-stored chilled finfish; DaFiF mackerel/tilapia/tuna common-name categories in Indonesia, with a separate nominal-4 °C rainbow trout fillet cohort. Indian origin to EU is a proposed scenario geography, not the geography of these measurements. MPEDA's FY2025-26 `CHILLED ITEMS` total confirms a broad chilled export category, but its separate EU total cannot prove chilled-finfish EU flows. Taxonomic precision must follow source evidence, never a translation guessed from a filename. Frozen fish, shrimp, smoked products and safety prediction need separate calibrations and are excluded from the primary model.

Acquisition timestamps, URLs, licenses, actual counts and limitations are maintained in `metadata/dataset_registry.csv` and `metadata/source_manifest.json`. A publicly available article is not evidence of unrestricted redistribution of every linked asset. Unknown licenses remain restricted to local research review until resolved.
