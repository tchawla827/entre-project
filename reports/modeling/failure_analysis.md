# Error analysis and stopping decision

The trout age-only model has the largest grouped-CV errors for the two late development cohorts: held-out day 4 is overpredicted and held-out day 5 is underpredicted. Measured cohort means fall from day 3 to day 4 and rise sharply at day 5. Quadratic ridge variants did not improve overall grouped MAE; their development results are in `trout_model_comparison.csv` and `trout_cv_folds.csv`. A flexible boosted model with only six development day groups would readily memorize the observed day curve and would not add an independently validated temperature or gas response. Tuning stopped after the bounded five-candidate comparison because the cohort/batch evidence ceiling dominates model complexity. The day-group bootstrap interval for baseline-minus-linear error crosses zero.

The smoked-catfish day-60 cell diagnostic is weakest for LDPE ambient peroxide (11.125 source-unit overprediction), reflecting late nonlinear oxidation. Coliform interpolation overall loses to carrying forward day 30. Since only one apparent processing study and two broad temperature conditions are available, fitting a higher-order package × temperature × time law would not establish cross-run or dynamic-temperature generalization. The curves are released as observations/interpolation, not as a selected thermal kinetic forecast.

DaFiF workbook clustering has moderate silhouette (0.305) and no observed specimen labels. Cluster assignment cannot be scored as freshness classification. The lack of true fish identities, product temperatures, independent shipments and directly linked outcomes cannot be fixed by more hyperparameter trials.
