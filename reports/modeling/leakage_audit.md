# Leakage and independence audit

The directly measured endpoints cannot be attached to DaFiF or trout gas readings at specimen level. `sensor_only` and `sensor_plus_known_context` supervised classifier feature views are therefore unavailable; fitting them to repeated cohort labels would make thousands of nearly identical rows appear independent. No row-wise sensor split or quality-label replication was performed. The DaFiF workbook exploration uses raw channel medians/IQR only, with one vector per workbook; it does not use local full-session z-scores or future-derived features.

Trout TVC is modeled with storage day only. The 21 assay fillets have 17 candidate original-fish IDs inferred from filename suffixes, pending author verification. One candidate ID occurs on both development and final day, so its development assay is embargoed. Within leave-one-day-out CV, training assays with any candidate ID present in the held-out day are excluded. The initial temporal holdout was inspected before this overlap was discovered, invalidating any claim of an untouched final test. A new independent cohort is required. Saved `trout_split_manifest.csv` and fold predictions permit audit. The very small effective sample and single study dominate uncertainty.

Catfish day zero is shared baseline, not 12 independent treatment starts. The six package × condition arms may share an unknown processing run. The day-60 check excludes entire treatment cells, but uses day-90 values to interpolate; it is not a prospective test. The published 4–5 °C and 25–30 °C conditions are ranges, not observed product-core temperature trajectories. No Q10, Arrhenius or variable-temperature fit is identifiable from these records.

The 2,298 local DaFiF session disagreements and the paper's three-row net count discrepancy are documented in `modeling_readiness.md` and Phase 1 reports. Neither local session field nor filename alone is a trustworthy group label; official folder metadata are used and conflicts retained. No source record was silently dropped for those disagreements.
