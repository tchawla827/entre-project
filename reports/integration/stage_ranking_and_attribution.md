# Physical stages, interventions and interactions

Physical stage decomposition integrates exposure over each stage's actual time boundary. In the air synthetic baseline, the 2 °C air indicator is landing 0, processing **3.9402**, cold storage **0.2362**, road 0, airport **5.2424**, flight **0.7567**, destination **4.8701 °C·h**, summing to **15.0455**. In the combined run, the corresponding values are 0, **3.9402**, **0.2362**, **54.4322**, **43.8426**, **20.7435**, **4.8882 °C·h**, summing to **128.0829**. Thermal carryover can place exposure in a later stage after an upstream event. [Stage table](../../results/integration/stage_contributions.parquet).

Intervention attribution is a different question. Exact Shapley over all eight coalitions of road, airport and flight interventions allocates the **113.0374 °C·h** air-route combined increase above 2 °C to road **55.7699**, airport **37.2676**, flight **19.9999 °C·h**. For the sea configuration, road **55.7699**, seaport **36.3841**, vessel **34.7802** sum to **126.9342 °C·h**. At 4 °C, the air road/airport/flight allocations are **46.5315/30.3084/9.4862 °C·h**. These are simulator-based allocations of configured interventions, not causal estimates of actual spoilage. [Attribution table](../../results/integration/intervention_attribution.parquet); [air plot](../../figures/integration/air_shapley.png); [sea plot](../../figures/integration/sea_shapley.png).

Combined minus the sum of the three individual effects yields a net interaction of **−0.0653 °C·h** for air and sea at 2 °C, and **+0.0282/+0.0275 °C·h** at 4 °C. Small net interaction does not eliminate downstream carryover: opposing local nonlinear effects can cancel in the route total. [Interaction table](../../results/integration/interactions.parquet).

To reduce the confounding of different event magnitudes, a separate *equal* +3 °C setpoint offset for 2 h starting one hour into each stage was run. In the air route at 2 °C, airport and flight each add **6.0000 °C·h**, processing **5.9305**, road **5.8108**, destination **5.4430**, landing **4.9613**, cold storage **2.9018**. At 4 °C, destination **3.7533** and airport **2.5001** lead, while cold storage adds zero. These standardized perturbations are fairer as an engineering comparison but still depend on each stage's baseline setpoint, neighboring stages and time available to recover. [Standardized table](../../results/integration/standardized_interventions.parquet); [figure](../../figures/integration/standardized_stage_events.png).
