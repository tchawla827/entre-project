# Bounded stress and ranking robustness

The Phase 4 runner reused Phase 3's paired Monte Carlo with fixed seed **2403**: **200 air** and **100 sea** draws. Each draw varies stage duration ×0.85–1.15, setpoint ±0.5 °C, ambient ±3 °C, air time constant 0.4–1.2 h, assumed product time constant 0.75–3 h, and event duration ×0.75–1.25. The same draw is applied to the baseline and intervention within a route. These are selected assumption ranges, **not parameter-confidence distributions or fish-spoilage prediction intervals**. [Draws](../../results/integration/sensitivity_draws.parquet), [rank draws](../../results/integration/ranking_draws.parquet), [histograms](../../figures/integration/assumption_draw_distributions.png).

For added *simulated air* degree-hours above 2 °C in the combined scenario, air draw median is **115.9022 °C·h** (5th/95th quantiles **83.8888/139.6812**); sea median is **130.2725** (**91.8932/159.9828**). Road is the largest configured one-stage intervention in **197/200** air draws at 2 °C and **193/200** at 4 °C, and in **100/100** sea draws at both references. Frequencies are conditional on the specified event magnitudes and draw bounds. The [convergence table](../../results/integration/monte_carlo_convergence.parquet) shows the air 2 °C combined median moving from **107.6053** at 25 draws to **115.9022** at 200, and sea from **119.7588** to **130.2725** at 100. This is a finite-sample diagnostic; no external validation follows from it. [Rank figure](../../figures/integration/road_rank_stability.png).

The *physical stage* with the most combined-case air exposure above 2 °C is road in **137/200** air draws and airport in **63/200**; in sea, road leads **75/100** and seaport **25/100**. This differs from the one-stage intervention-effect ranking because physical boundaries include upstream carryover and ambient exposure within the stage. The [stage-rank draws](../../results/integration/stage_rank_draws.parquet) and [physical-rank figure](../../figures/integration/physical_stage_rank_stability.png) show this distinction. Across all 48 actual run traces, [physical_stage_rankings.parquet](../../results/integration/physical_stage_rankings.parquet) identifies top stages or records undefined rank when route exposure is zero.

Low/central/high corner profiles simultaneously change duration, setpoint, ambient, response times and event length, so they are deliberately aggressive **hypothetical stress tests**, not confidence bounds. Air combined added 2 °C exposure: **69.0180**, **113.0374**, **161.4560 °C·h**; sea: **77.9610**, **126.9342**, **185.8205**. [Stress table](../../results/integration/stress_profiles.parquet). The Phase 3 magnitude tests show important ranking reversals: a 0.5 h road failure makes airport largest at 2 and 4 °C; an 8 h, +8 °C flight event makes flight largest at 2 °C. Equal-magnitude tests also change the top stage. Therefore “road is always dominant” is unsupported; the correct claim is conditional on configured interventions and reference.

The 15-minute to 5-minute Phase 3 baseline integration difference was **0.0635%** for the 2 °C air indicator. Replaying nonfish proxy residual shapes shifts baseline/combined values modestly but does not validate route temperatures. Because the assumed-product lag affects its own channel while the air channel is computed separately, lag sensitivity cannot be interpreted as fish-core calibration.
