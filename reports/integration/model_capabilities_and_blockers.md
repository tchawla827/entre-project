# Biological model capabilities and blockers

The machine-readable [capability matrix](../../results/integration/capability_matrix.json) identifies the only enabled tasks. Phase 2 trout TVC is a storage-age-only, internally evaluated nominal-4 °C study diagnostic, with no counterfactual temperature effect and no untouched final test. Smoked-catfish endpoints interpolate within observed categorical treatment/packaging/day cells; they do not describe fresh fish or changing shipment temperature. DaFiF workbook clusters are descriptive patterns, not freshness classes. The planned Gradient Boosting fresh-fish spoilage classifier remains **BLOCKED** because specimen-matched labels and independent biological units are absent.

The service verifies the saved Phase 2 artifact hashes before loading the two limited biological branches. Missing or changed artifacts raise `ArtifactUnavailableError`; unknown domains raise `IntegrationInputError` or Phase 2 `UnsupportedCalibrationError`. Requests for fresh-fish variable-temperature quality or calibrated spoilage probability propagate `UnsupportedCalibrationError`. Exposure is returned only when explicitly requested as `thermal_exposure`; its numeric °C·h is not converted into a probability.

No new eligible biological dataset was found in the [targeted public re-audit](public_data_reaudit.md). Minimum new evidence: specimen/lot-linked fish-core temperature plus initial/final repeated quality assays over multiple temperatures, documented methods, batch independence, and independent Indian shipment outcomes. The proposed [field linkage schema](../../configs/integration/field_linkage_schema.yaml) specifies IDs and measurements needed to avoid invalid joins. A pilot should determine independent sample size and reserve whole exporters/routes or later shipments for final testing. Until these gates pass, individual spoilage risk, shelf-life, regulatory compliance and field reliability remain **NOT_CALIBRATED/BLOCKED**.
