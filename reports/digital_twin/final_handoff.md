# Phase 3 final handoff — thermal-exposure digital twin

**Status (2026-10-28): ENGINEERING COMPLETE; BIOLOGICAL PREDICTION BLOCKED.** This is a reproducible, stage-based *simulation* of chilled-finfish cold-chain air temperature and an explicitly assumed product-lag channel. The reported °C·h are thermal-exposure indicators. They are not spoilage probabilities, freshness classifications, shelf-life estimates, regulatory compliance decisions, or observed Indian shipment outcomes. No fresh-finfish variable-temperature deterioration kinetics or independent paired shipment validation exist in the available data.

## Context, route, and evidence boundary

The air example is a hypothetical Kochi-to-EU chilled-finfish export. The local MPEDA FY2025–26 table reports **13,056.73922 metric tonnes** for chilled items across all markets, **297,517.86592** for all products to the EU, and **460.42683** for all items in the Cochin Air Cargo ACC category. These are *separate marginals*, not a chilled-finfish × Kochi × EU cross-tab, and do not identify a shipment, route, travel time, or temperature. The sea example is even more conditional: the table has a Cochin SEZ cargo category, but no “Cochin sea” category or evidence of a 14-day fish voyage. See [data audit](data_audit.json), [data assumptions](data_and_assumptions.md), and the Phase 1 [scientific sufficiency report](../data/scientific_sufficiency_report.md).

The [air configuration](../../configs/digital_twin/experiment.yaml) defines seven contiguous stages, all **illustrative assumptions**: landing/icing 3 h at 1.5 °C; processing 5 h at 3 °C; cold storage 12 h at 1 °C; road 5 h at 2 °C; airport 6 h at 3 °C; flight 12 h at 2 °C; EU arrival 4 h at 3.5 °C. Baseline total: **47 h**. The [sea overlay](../../configs/digital_twin/sea_route.yaml) substitutes a 10 h seaport stage at 2.5 °C and a 336 h refrigerated-vessel stage at 1.5 °C, for **375 h** total. No biological viability claim is made for that duration. Route timestamps begin at an arbitrary UTC epoch; they are scenario clocks, not actual departure dates. Species names are selectable metadata only; no species-specific dynamics are claimed. Sources and ranges are in the config and [assumptions report](data_and_assumptions.md).

## Engine and reproducibility

`src/digital_twin/` provides validated shipment/route/stage/event/sample/run/result dataclasses, a UTC stage builder, exact first-order air transitions, a separate assumed first-order product lag, continuous-clock nonfish proxy replay, interval-weighted exposure integration, stage/event summaries, scenario runner, exact three-intervention Shapley attribution, bounded Monte Carlo, Phase 2 capability/refusal adapter, CLI, and figures. An event can temporarily change the setpoint, turn refrigeration off toward ambient, or open a door with assumed faster air exchange. Air and assumed-product states carry across stages. The numerical endpoint series is integrated by linear interpolation of interval endpoints; the state dynamics at interval endpoints are exact for constant targets. The sample interval is 15 minutes, split at event boundaries. There is no fitted heat-transfer coefficient or measured fish core.

Use the pinned repository environment:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m src.digital_twin.cli simulate --scenario baseline
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m src.digital_twin.cli simulate --all
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m src.digital_twin.cli simulate --all --route sea
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m src.digital_twin.cli analyze --all
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m src.digital_twin.cli validate --all
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest tests/digital_twin tests/models tests/data_pipeline -q
```

`analyze --all` runs synthetic air and two nonfish-proxy-shaped air configurations, 200 paired assumption draws, exact Shapley attribution, magnitude/threshold stress tests, independent verification and nine PNG figures. The [reproducibility manifest](reproducibility_manifest.json) records Python/package versions, source/data/model/config SHA-256 digests; `run_id` hashes resolved scenario inputs, seed, and proxy metadata. The source checkout has no Git repository, so a Git commit SHA is unavailable; file hashes provide the code revision record.

## Temperature inputs and output definitions

Synthetic air uses a 0.75 h response time; opening a door uses an assumed 0.2 h maximum response time. The independent illustrative product channel uses a 1.5 h lag and starts at 1 °C; air starts at 1.5 °C. Ambient 22–30 °C, event lengths, stage durations, and setpoints are hypothetical. For proxy replay, citrus source `10C_OP.csv:P1_mean` (package-position, **not** container air or fish) is trimmed after the first 48 h, detrended against a centered 24 h rolling mean, and its half-hour residuals are cycled on the *continuous route clock* around the synthetic stage setpoint. Cold-room `SENSOR02` (unknown placement/timezone) supplies a stable 24 h window after 120 h, resampled to 15 minutes and median centered; earlier anomalous windows are excluded. Both transformed traces are shape/variability examples, never observations at the modeled route. See [proxy manifests](proxy_replay_manifests.json).

For a reference `r` °C, air degree-hours are `∫ max(T_air(t) − r, 0) dt` in °C·h, with elapsed time in hours. Hours above reference integrate crossing fractions within intervals. The 2 °C and 4 °C references are **scenario-comparison settings**, not a universal legal or biological threshold. The product-lag indicator uses the separately named estimated-product channel. Route and stage exposure percentages are duration weighted; stage fractions are null if total route exposure is zero. Event-window extra exposure compares the event interval to the same stage-local clock in baseline; combined-event windows can contain carryover from upstream events. Route-level intervention differences include both event effects and added time. Reported exposure per route hour helps compare different-duration scenarios.

Schema version **1**: `results/digital_twin/{synthetic,proxy_citrus,proxy_coldroom,sea_synthetic}/` contains `scenario_summary.csv`, `stage_exposure.csv`, `event_window_deltas.csv` (air analysis modes), `{scenario}_trace.parquet`, and `{scenario}_metrics.json`. Each trace row has UTC interval endpoints, stage ID, air and estimated-product start/end temperatures, source type, event kind, run ID, and schema version. Metrics JSON has run inputs, nested route/stage/event quantities, provenance, and capability statuses. `results/digital_twin/assumption_draws.parquet` contains paired Monte Carlo input/output rows. A later dashboard can read these files without executing the simulator. Sea-route scenario names differ (`seaport_delay_door`, `vessel_excursion`); the air-only analysis and Shapley routine are deliberately not applied to sea.

## Actual air-route results

All numbers below are **15-minute synthetic air** results above 2 °C unless stated otherwise. Baseline: **15.0455 °C·h** in 47 h; no degree-hours above 4 °C. One-stage additional °C·h versus baseline: processing delay **2.9993** (+3 h), storage +3 °C **24.5202**, road refrigeration off 2 h **55.8025**, airport delay +3 h and door open 0.75 h **37.3003**, flight +5 °C for 4 h **19.9999**, destination delay +3 h **4.4946**. The combined road/airport/flight scenario lasts 50 h and reaches **128.0829 °C·h**, an increase of **113.0374 °C·h**; its 4 °C indicator is **86.3262 °C·h**. The baseline 2 °C exposure per route hour is **0.3201 °C** versus **2.5617 °C** for combined. See [scenario results](scenario_results.md) and [scenario CSV](../../results/digital_twin/synthetic/scenario_summary.csv).

Baseline stage decomposition (°C·h above 2 °C): landing **0**, processing **3.9402**, cold storage **0.2362**, road **0**, airport **5.2424**, flight **0.7567**, destination **4.8701**; sum **15.0455**. In the combined scenario: landing **0**, processing **3.9402**, storage **0.2362**, road **54.4322**, airport **43.8426**, flight **20.7435**, destination **4.8882**; sum **128.0829**. These physical-stage totals differ from *intervention attribution* because thermal state carries downstream. Exact Shapley across all eight coalitions of road failure, airport delay/door, and flight excursion allocates the combined increase: road **55.7699**, airport **37.2676**, flight **19.9999 °C·h**; contributions sum to **113.0374**. See [attribution](stage_attribution.md) and [exact results](exact_shapley.json).

The hypothetical 375 h sea baseline yields **13.4640 °C·h** above 2 °C; its 8 h vessel setpoint excursion adds **34.7802 °C·h**, and combined road/seaport/vessel disruption adds **126.9342 °C·h**. This compares *exposure under configured setpoints*, not transport suitability or product quality. The long below-reference vessel time is why route duration alone does not force the 2 °C indicator upward; no degradation model allows a shelf-life conclusion. See [sea summary](../../results/digital_twin/sea_synthetic/scenario_summary.csv).

## Uncertainty, verification, and scientific limits

Two hundred paired draws (seed **2403**) vary stage duration ×0.85–1.15, setpoint ±0.5 °C, ambient ±3 °C, air response 0.4–1.2 h, assumed product lag 0.75–3 h, and event length ×0.75–1.25. Combined additional air exposure above 2 °C has median **115.9022** and empirical 5th/95th draw quantiles **83.8888/139.6812 °C·h**. These are *assumption-distribution summaries*, not fish-spoilage prediction intervals. Road is the largest one-stage increase in **197/200** draws at 2 °C, **193/200** at 4 °C. A 0.5 h road interruption makes the airport scenario largest at both references; an 8 h +8 °C flight excursion makes flight largest at 2 °C. See [sensitivity report](sensitivity_and_uncertainty.md).

The engine verifies deterministic replay, exact stage sum within numerical tolerance, warming monotonicity under a fixed schedule, zero-duration behavior, timestamp-gap/duplicate rejection, and noncalibrated inference refusal. The 15-to-5-minute baseline degree-hour difference is **0.0635%**. The full suite passed **24 tests** (two dependency deprecation warnings), and the four baseline Parquet modes were loaded independently. Full tests and load checks are documented in [verification](verification_and_validation.md). The selected proxy windows alter baseline 2 °C exposure to **15.431** (citrus) and **15.874** (cold-room) °C·h, illustrating shape sensitivity only. Source data do not validate the route or biology.

`request_quality_trajectory` forwards to Phase 2 and propagates `UnsupportedCalibrationError`; `request_spoilage_probability` raises the same exception. Capabilities mark trout age-only TVC and smoked-catfish treatment tables LIMITED in their original scope, DaFiF clusters DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY, and variable-temperature fresh-finfish quality and spoilage probability NOT_CALIBRATED. No exposure field is converted to a probability. Phase 4 must obtain specimen-linked fresh-finfish quality endpoints across temperatures, measured fish-core and air histories, and independent Indian shipments with quality outcomes; fit kinetics with grouped validation, preserve an untouched shipment-level test set, then implement a versioned calibrated quality adapter. Until then, **validated fish spoilage prediction and independent field validation remain BLOCKED**. See [scientific limitations](scientific_limitations.md).
