# Scientific scope and blocked claims

**Supported:** repeatable simulation of hypothetical stage-level chilled-finfish *air* thermal exposure, an explicitly assumed product-temperature lag, and operational perturbation sensitivity. The chosen 2 and 4 °C references are configurable comparison settings; neither is asserted as a universal fish safety limit or legal compliance threshold. Degree-hours and hours above reference do not encode microbial growth or shelf life.

**Approximate evidence only:** citrus refrigerated-container package-position data and generic cold-room sensor data supply transformed residual *shape*. They are not fish-core, actual route-air, Indian export, or biological-validation datasets. MPEDA values are aggregate FY marginals without product × market × port cells or shipment timing. Route durations, setpoints, ambient conditions, thermal response times, door behavior, and events are modeled assumptions. Monte Carlo quantiles quantify variation under selected assumption bounds, not uncertainty in actual Indian shipments.

**Blocked:** fresh-finfish variable-temperature quality trajectories, calibrated spoilage probabilities, species-specific kinetics, remaining shelf-life estimates, claims of route suitability, regulatory pass/fail, and independent shipment-level performance. DaFiF has 27,120 sensor observations without specimen-matched freshness assays; trout has 21 individual TVC assays at nominal 4 °C and no direct link to its 25,200 sensor readings; smoked-catfish curves apply only to their treatment/package/day cells. None provides a validated chilled-finfish variable-temperature model. There are zero real Indian export shipments with paired temperature histories and measured spoilage outcomes. See the [Phase 2 handoff](../modeling/final_handoff.md).

Phase 4 requires a prespecified species/product/endpoints protocol; specimen-linked, repeated temperature–quality observations at multiple temperatures; measured air and fish-core histories for transfer-function calibration; and independent shipments with baseline quality plus final outcome. Evaluation must group by fish/lot/shipment, reserve an untouched shipment-level test set, report calibration/uncertainty and failure modes, and then version an adapter for `predict_quality_trajectory`. Until those data are collected, `UnsupportedCalibrationError` is the correct behavior.
