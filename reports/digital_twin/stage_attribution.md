# Stage and intervention attribution

**Physical-stage decomposition** sums the integral of `max(air °C − 2 °C, 0)` over each stage's own time boundary. In the 47 h synthetic baseline, landing 0, processing 3.9402, cold storage 0.2362, road 0, airport 5.2424, flight 0.7567, and destination 4.8701 °C·h total 15.0455. In the 50 h combined scenario, the same stage order gives 0, 3.9402, 0.2362, 54.4322, 43.8426, 20.7435, and 4.8882 °C·h, totaling 128.0829. Small effects in later stages can reflect upstream thermal carryover. See [stage CSV](../../results/digital_twin/synthetic/stage_exposure.csv) and [figure](../../figures/digital_twin/stage_exposure_decomposition.png).

**One-stage intervention** runs each disruption alone against the configured baseline. At 2 °C, additional °C·h rank road 55.8025, airport 37.3003, storage 24.5202, flight 19.9999, destination 4.4946, processing 2.9993. This ranks *specified intervention magnitudes*, not inherent biological risk of stages. Three of the scenarios change total duration, so their absolute difference includes added baseline-temperature exposure. Route-hour normalized metrics are saved beside absolute values.

**Combined-effect allocation** enumerates all `2³ = 8` coalitions of road failure, airport delay/door, and flight excursion, always comparing the same reference and baseline. Exact Shapley contributions to the 113.0374 °C·h increase are road 55.7699, airport 37.2676, flight 19.9999. They sum exactly, allowing thermal carryover and nonlinear interactions to be shared over coalition order. There is no Shapley sampling error for these three players. The result is an accounting identity of the configured simulator, not evidence of causes in real shipments. The algorithm and raw numbers are in [exact_shapley.json](exact_shapley.json).

The dominant intervention is conditional. Reducing road interruption to 0.5 h makes airport largest at both 2 and 4 °C references. A flight +8 °C excursion for 8 h makes flight largest at 2 °C but road remains largest at 4 °C. [Magnitude rankings](event_magnitude_rankings.csv) and [stability figure](../../figures/digital_twin/dominant_stage_stability.png) expose this instability.
