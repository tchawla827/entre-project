# Accessibility and responsive check

Chromium screenshots were reviewed at 1440 × 900 desktop and 390 × 844 mobile. The final mobile page has a collapsed sidebar and no horizontal document overflow. All five controls have visible labels; the tab and download controls are standard Streamlit widgets. The temperature curve, stage bars and comparison bars use a consistent teal accent against white, with units on the axes and the numeric values available above the chart or in a collapsed table.

The main page was reduced to the simulation and one comparison view. The scientific scope line is visible near the title without dominating the results. Method detail and the stage table are collapsed. The theme uses dark text on white, a pale sidebar and teal active controls. These checks are visual and interaction smoke checks; a formal screen-reader and keyboard-only audit remains open.
